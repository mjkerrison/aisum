What's an unconference?

An unconference is much like a normal conference, but most of the agenda isn't set in advance. Attendees pitch sessions they want to run or want to have someone run, everyone votes on what they'd like to go to, and then someone (in this case us, the organisers) does some constrained optimisation to build a program.

What this means for you: you may want to think about what you might like to run, what you'd like to see others run, and how you might make the most of the opportunity to connect with others in the community.
